# Rewrite

The request points at an existing text: an earlier answer, a draft, or a report. A rewrite keeps every rule in SKILL.md and adds these.

Before you write, count the words of the original. The rewrite has a **budget** of one and a half times that count, unless the user asks for more. Spend the budget on glosses first, then on the basics a passage cannot be understood without.

- Keep the original's headings, structure, tables, and diagrams.
- Each gloss is a phrase inside the existing sentence. Basics go inside the existing paragraphs.
- Bring in a fact from elsewhere in the material only when the passage cannot be understood without it.
- After the text, list what stayed Open, limited to what changes what the reader decides or does. The list counts against the budget.

## Check

- Count the words of the rewrite, including the Open list. The count is within the budget, or the user asked for more. Over budget, cut and count again.
- Every section, table, and diagram in the rewrite was in the original.
- Every addition is a gloss or a basic the outsider needed.
