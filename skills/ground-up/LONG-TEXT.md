# Long text

A long text is a document, a report, an onboarding guide, or an explanation from zero. It keeps every rule in SKILL.md and adds these.

- Write headings that state the idea in full words, so a reader who scans only the headings can follow the story. A heading that says who does what, and when, works on its own. A heading that names a topic makes the reader open the body.
- When a section uses a term glossed in a distant section, repeat a short gloss.
- Put what is decided and what is pending in separate sections. A final list of Open items is fine.
- Use a table for data with real columns, such as component and responsibility.
- When the text describes a flow with three or more moving parts, add a Mermaid diagram beside the prose. The prose still explains the flow.

## Check

- Each heading makes sense on its own, without the body and without earlier sections.
- A term glossed far away gets a short gloss again where it is used.
- Every heading, table, and diagram holds content that needs that form.
